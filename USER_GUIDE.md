# User guide — NexSend

A simple way to send things to people at the institute. No email attachments, no
WhatsApp compression — the file arrives exactly as it was.

## Signing in

Use the username and password your administrator gave you. Your session lasts
30 days on that device, so you will not be asked every time.

There is no sign-up. Only the administrator creates accounts.

## Sending

1. **Send to** — tap the people who should receive it. Tap again to unselect.
   The green dot means they are online right now.
2. Choose **Text** or **Files**.

**Text** — type or paste anything: a link, a code, a note. There is no length limit.
The **Paste from clipboard** button fills the box with whatever you copied last.

**Files** — drag and drop, or use **Choose files** / **Choose folder**.
Images, videos, documents and whole folders all work. A folder is zipped in your
browser and sent as one file. Original quality is kept, up to 2 GB.
3. **Send**. A progress bar shows the upload; the receiver sees it arrive at the
same time.
4. To send to one person, pick one name. To send to several, pick several.

### Favorites

Tap the star next to a name to make them a favorite. In **Settings** you can set
favorites — or everyone — to be accepted automatically, so nothing waits for a tap.

## Receiving

New transfers appear in **Incoming** with a count badge and a small notification.
Anywhere in the app you will see it pop up.

- **Accept** — the file is ready to download.
- **Decline** — the sender sees it was declined.
- **Download** — saves the original file.

Text arrives instantly, no download needed: read it and press **Copy**.

## History

**History** lists everything you sent and received, with a search box and filters.

- Files can be downloaded again for **1 hour** after they were sent.
- After that the row stays, marked **Expired**, but the file itself is gone.
- The trash icon removes an entry from your list. The administrator keeps the record.

## Settings

- **Profile picture** — tap **Add photo**, pick any image; it is cropped to a square
  and stored with your account. Nobody else can change it, including the administrator.
  **Remove** puts your initials back.
- **Display name** — this is what others see.
- **Username and password** — set by the administrator. Ask them to change it.
- **Receiving** — ask me every time (default), auto-accept favorites, or auto-accept everyone.
- **Favorites** — the same stars as on the Send screen.

## Good to know

- The site is dark only, and works on phone, tablet and desktop.
- Files are deleted an hour after sending, so download what you need.
- Everything you send goes only to the people you picked.
